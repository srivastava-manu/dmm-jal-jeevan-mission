Jal Jeevan Mission

Digital Maturity
Model Survey Form

A printable assessment form for reviewing digital capability across rural drinking water services.

Current model v2.3 · 36 capabilities · 6 layers · 0–4 scale

Assessment details

How to complete this form

  1. Read each capability question and select one maturity score from 0 to 4.
  2. Base the score on what is in routine use at the State/UT scale, not only on a sanctioned plan or pilot.
  3. Use the evidence / notes area to record the system name, coverage, source, or actions needed.
  4. Answer every capability. The complete assessment contains 36 responses and has a maximum score of 144.
0 Does not exist
No digital system for this capability. Handled on paper, in spreadsheets, or not at all.
1 Under development
A system has been sanctioned, procured or is in development. Nothing is in productive use yet.
2 Pilot complete
Live and working with real data in a limited setting — one or a few districts, or a small user group — for at least one full reporting cycle. Not yet rolled out further.
3 Functional at limited scale
In routine production use across part of the state by the intended users. Gaps remain — some districts still manual, or some workflow steps still offline.
4 Fully functional at state scale
In routine use across effectively all districts by effectively all intended users. The data is authoritative, no parallel manual register is maintained, and the system is owned and monitored.

This form mirrors the published v2.3 assessment model. For the digital workflow, use the Jal Jeevan Mission Digital Maturity Model application.
